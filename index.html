Frequently asked questions

How do I pair with my partner?

One of you taps Invite my partner and shares the 6-character code. The other taps I have a code and enters it. You then share the same map.

Can my partner see my photos?

Not yet. Photos stay on the iPhone that added them. Your partner sees your pins, ratings and notes, but not your photos.

Does one subscription cover both of us?

Yes. Lovepins Premium is shared by the couple: one of you subscribes, both of you get it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Lovepins. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app (header › Our space).

How do I leave a couple or delete my account?

Tap the header at the top of the map › Our space › Leave our couple or Delete my account. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.
