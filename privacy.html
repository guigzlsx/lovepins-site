Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Lovepins is a private map for couples. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it for us, and how to delete it. The short version: we keep only what the app needs to sync your shared map between your two phones. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos you add to a pin are stored only on the iPhone that added them. They are not uploaded to our servers, so your partner does not see them.
  • Your location is used on your device to center the map and find date ideas nearby. We never send or store your location on our servers. To find places, the app uses Apple Maps search, which Apple processes under its own privacy policy.
  • Reminders (anniversaries, "one year ago today") are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the app:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, real-time sync), hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-1).
  • Resend, to send the sign-in code to your email address.
  • RevenueCat, to manage subscription status across your two phones.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, maps and place search.

Each provider processes data on our behalf and under its own security and privacy commitments.

Who can see your data

Your pins, your first name and your couple's start date are visible only to you and your partner. Access is enforced on our database for every request. We access data only when needed to operate the service or when you ask us for help.

Retention

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete a pin, it disappears from both phones right away. It is kept on our servers in deleted form so the deletion syncs to your partner's phone, and it is erased with your couple's data.
  • If you leave your couple, the pins you added stay on the map you shared with your partner. Your app starts fresh and the data stored on your iPhone for that couple is erased.
  • If you delete your account, your account, email address and first name are deleted from our servers. If your partner is still in the couple, the shared pins stay on their map without your name. If you were alone, your couple and all its pins are deleted.
  • Short-lived technical logs are deleted automatically by our hosting provider.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap the header at the top of the map → Our space → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Lovepins' access to your Apple ID. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: manage it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

You can also email us at hello@lovepins.app to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

Children

Lovepins is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
